Property types

What kind of property do you manage?

The same maintenance visit looks different in an occupied apartment block, a warehouse and a shop under fit-out.

Apartment buildings

If the work involves a shared corridor or service cupboard, tell us who holds the keys and when residents can be given access notices.

Warehouses

Send the unit number, working hours and the section of the warehouse involved. Mention high ceilings or racking that affects access to overhead pipework.

Retail & office fit-outs

A marked-up floor plan is a good starting point. Highlight new partitions, planned ceiling closures and any existing equipment that falls inside the proposed works.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

Our head office is in Dubai and we accept enquiries from all seven emirates. Include the property location so we can discuss visit arrangements.

Can I request installation and maintenance together?

Yes. Tell us which equipment is already installed and which parts of the project involve new work.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the service required, site location, building type and preferred timing. Available photos, drawings or previous service notes can help clarify the scope.

How do I arrange a site visit?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp our team on +971 50 980 9255. The team will discuss access and confirm the next available visit.
