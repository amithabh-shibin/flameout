FlameOut Electromechanical LLC

Established in 2026, FlameOut Electromechanical LLC is a maintenance and technical services company based in Dubai. We provide dependable, high-quality maintenance at prices our customers can afford.

We specialise in fire alarm systems, fire fighting systems, emergency lighting systems, and mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) maintenance across the UAE.

Our mission

To make professional maintenance accessible by bringing together excellent workmanship, competitive pricing and dependable service.

Our vision

We believe quality maintenance should be affordable. Our aim is to give every customer lasting value while keeping safety and quality at the centre of our work.

Our commitment

Your safety, comfort and satisfaction are our priorities. We aim to earn your trust over time through clear communication, transparent pricing and professional service.

How we work

From enquiry to site visit

01

Describe the site

Tell us the building type, location and services involved. Photos or drawings can clarify access and equipment.

02

Agree the scope

Discuss the areas to be visited, any occupied spaces and the timing that works for the property.

03

Coordinate the work

Arrange site access with your contact and discuss findings, follow-up tasks or future maintenance.

Site coordination

Planning a visit around the people on site

In an occupied building, getting to the equipment can take as much coordination as arranging the visit. A facilities contact may have the pump room key, while a tenant controls access to the ceiling space above a shop.

When you speak to our team, mention who can meet the team, where contractors should enter and which rooms are available. For fit-out work, include the date the ceiling is due to close so access can be discussed early.
