Scope of work

What to include in a maintenance enquiry

These details help the team distinguish a local issue from work that may involve several floors or systems.

Pump room equipment

Include the pump or controller reference from your maintenance records and describe what changed. If the issue was recorded during a previous service visit, include the relevant note.

Corridor equipment

Use the floor and nearest unit number to identify the cabinet, detector or call point. If several items need attention, list their locations separately.

Water supply and drainage

Mention whether the issue affects one fixture, several units or a shared service area. Add when you first noticed it and whether the area is currently accessible.
